        How it works

        Running a race

        1. Pick the race type at the top: Bronze 10, Bronze 8, Silver or Gold. The race and lap times are shown under each one.
        2. Tap Start. A countdown runs (beeps and voice), then the air horn starts the race.
        3. Each lap gets a chime and a callout ("Lap 3"). The last one is "Final lap". The big number is laps completed.
        4. A brass fanfare ends the race when the time is up.

        During a race

        • Pause freezes the clock. Resume gives a 3-2-1 before it carries on.
        • Hold Stop for about a second to end the race and reset to zero. A quick tap does nothing, so it can't be stopped by accident.
        • Lap sounds can be switched off or on at any time.

        Heats

        Use Heats to line up a run of races. Pick a break between heats and the next one starts on its own, announced as "Heat 2", "Heat 3"… Or choose manual start.

        Log

        Every finished or stopped race is saved on this phone. Share it or download a spreadsheet (CSV) from Log.

        Settings

        Volume, voice (Liam, Classic or a phone voice), countdown length or Off, time warnings, a high-contrast Sunlight theme, and your own custom race types.

        Tips

        • Bluetooth speaker: pair it in your phone's settings first. The app plays through whatever is connected. Use Test sound to check the volume.
        • Install it: iPhone, Share then Add to Home Screen. Android, ⋮ then Install app. Mac Safari, File then Add to Dock. It then works offline at the field.
        • Keep the app open during a race. It keeps the screen awake for you.
